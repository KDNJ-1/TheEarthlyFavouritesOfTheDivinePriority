### 2. Impact & Novelty Statement for Aavishkar / Anveshan / Fellowships (Feature K4)
* **Taxonomic & Methodological Novelty:** Resolves vernacular search ambiguity and replaces unvalidated single-molecule docking with a **5-layer Evidence Triangulation pipeline** (PRISMA + Open-License Curation + ADMET Partitioning + ROC-AUC Benchmarked Docking + Bilingual Open-Source Web Portal).
* **Formulation Chemistry Insight:** Mechanistically bridges Indian Knowledge Systems (*Swarasa*, *Bhavana*, *Taila*, *Ghrita*) with modern lipophilicity (`logP`) and polar surface area (`TPSA`) constraints—showing why water extracts deliver polar C-glycosides (*Orientin*, *Vitexin*) while lipid bases (*Taila/Ghrita*) are mandatory to extract non-polar wound-healing lignans (*Pinoresinol dimethyl ether*) and sterols (*Beta-sitosterol*).
