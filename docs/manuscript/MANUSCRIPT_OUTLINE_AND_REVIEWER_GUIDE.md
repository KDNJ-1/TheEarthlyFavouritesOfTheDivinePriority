3. **Results:**

   - **Section 3.3:** Benchmarked docking against MMP-9 (`1GKC`) and COX-2 (`5IKR`).
4. **Discussion & Limitations:** Synergistic polypharmacology and future wet-lab enzymatic ($IC_{50}$) validation roadmap.
