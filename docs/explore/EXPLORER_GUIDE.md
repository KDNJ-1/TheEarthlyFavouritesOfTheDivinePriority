# 🎮 Explorer Mode Guide: The Story of Sacred Durva (*Cynodon dactylon*)

### 1. The Lock-and-Key Story in 60 Seconds
When skin is injured, an enzyme called **MMP-9** can become overactive and chew up the collagen scaffolding needed to close the wound, while **COX-2** drives painful swelling.
* **The Locks:** `MMP-9 (PDB: 1GKC)` and `COX-2 (PDB: 5IKR)`.
* **The Durva Keys:** Flavonoids like **Luteolin** (`-9.1 kcal/mol`) and **Orientin** (`-8.9 kcal/mol`) fit snugly into the MMP-9 pocket, while **p-Coumaric acid** (`0.48%` of shoots) shields DNA from free-radical damage!

### 2. Printable One-Page Poster Summary (Feature B9)
* **Title:** Sacred Grass Under the Digital Microscope: Triangulating *Cynodon dactylon*
* **Problem:** Traditional formulations (*Durva Swarasa*, *Durva Taila*, *Durva Ghrita*) show remarkable wound-healing and cytoprotective effects in wet-lab models, but their multi-target atomic mechanisms remain scattered.
* **Discovery:** Flavone aglycones (*Luteolin*, *Apigenin*) provide high oral/topical permeability and dual `MMP-9`/`COX-2` inhibition, while C-glycosides (*Orientin*, *Vitexin*) drive local angiogenesis at `10 µg/mL`.
